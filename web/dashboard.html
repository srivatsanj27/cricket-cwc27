      How it works

      Every men's ODI since 2019 updates an Elo rating for each team, with home advantage. Match predictions come straight from the ratings. The World Cup is played 10,000 times in the ICC's 2027 format, Qualifier included, with each team's rating blurred for the time left before the tournament, because ratings drift over a year.

      Data from Cricsheet, plus results entered by hand where Cricsheet has none.
