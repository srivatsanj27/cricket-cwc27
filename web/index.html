How it works

Each team has an Elo rating built from every men's ODI since 2019. A gap of 400 points means 10 to 1 odds. Playing in your own country adds 80 points for that match, which is the setting that best predicted past results.

The series is played 20,000 times with each match's win chance. Ties and washouts are left out, so every match has a winner. Only teams that have played an ODI since the 2023 World Cup final are listed.
